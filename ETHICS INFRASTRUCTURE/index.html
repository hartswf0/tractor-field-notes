Ethics Infrastructure
Watson Hartsoe, PhD Candidate, Digital Media, Georgia Tech

This project explores language as leverage point for spatial computing. Prompts become executable specifications; authorship shifts from engine primitives to cultural, narrative, and spatial intent.

Four Hubs:

  • TRACTOR : The workhorse. Field notes, trails, and patterns.
  • CORE-AGE : The engine. Semantic-to-spatial compilation.
  • ATG 3.0 : Associative Terrain Graphs.
  • Role Deck : Early prompting experiments.

Navigation: Each hub has a Map, Gloss, Genome, Index, and Repo. Top links: Paper, Planning, WAG Work, Playlist.
