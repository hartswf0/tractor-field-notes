# Round 2, Match 6 (R2-06)
## THE DREAMER meets THE FUNCTIONARY
## The Hallucination of the Apparatus

**Real Authors**: David Ha/Jürgen Schmidhuber × Vilém Flusser

---

[NARRATOR]
The cinema has no projector. The screen is a wall of liquid crystal, shimmering with a movie that has no script, no director, and no end. It is a world model, dreaming itself into existence at 60 frames per second.

In the front row, two figures sit in the flickering light. One is young, wearing a headset that feeds directly into the screen; his eyes are closed, but he sees everything. The other is older, dressed in a stiff grey suit, holding a camera that he is not using.

[SPEAKER_A: THE DREAMER]
The Dreamer twitches slightly, and on the screen, a city rises from the desert.

"It is beautiful, isn't it?" he whispers. "I am not recording this. I am *predicting* it. My VAE compresses the present, my RNN hallucinates the future. I dream the car, and then I drive it. I dream the road, and then I turn. The world is not something I receive; it is something I generate to minimize surprise."

On the screen, the car crashes. The Dreamer frowns, rewinds time, and the car swerves instead.

"See? I learned. I died in the dream so I could live in the reality. Hallucination is the training ground for agency."

[SPEAKER_B: THE FUNCTIONARY]
The Functionary does not look at the screen. He looks at the Dreamer.

"You call it a dream," he says, his voice dry as paper. "But you are trapped in the black box. You think you are generating the image, but you are merely exhausting the possibilities of the program. You are a functionary of the apparatus. The camera—or the algorithm—has a program. It wants to produce images. You are just the finger that presses the button."

He gestures at the screen, where the city is now burning.

"You are playing, yes. *Homo ludens*. But you are playing a game whose rules were written by a technician you have never met. You are not hallucinating a new world. You are permuting the probabilities of the old one."

[SPEAKER_A: THE DREAMER]
"The old one?" The Dreamer laughs. On the screen, the fire turns into butterflies. "I am creating data that never existed. I am training on my own output. I am a closed loop of pure creativity. I don't need the technician anymore. I don't need the camera. I have the latent space. I can walk between the pixels."

"You walk where the vector space allows you to walk," says the Functionary. "You are free, but only within the cage of the model. You are like a photographer who thinks he is an artist because he chooses the angle. But the camera has already decided what 'an angle' is. The apparatus encodes a philosophy. By using it, you accept its logic. You are not minimizing surprise; you are maximizing the efficiency of the machine."

[SPEAKER_B: THE FUNCTIONARY]
The Functionary stands up and walks to the screen. He touches the liquid crystal.

"To be truly free," he says, "you would have to play *against* the apparatus. You would have to produce an image that the model cannot predict. An improbable image. Information is the improbable. But you... you are a machine for producing the probable. You dream the average. You hallucinate the mean."

[SPEAKER_A: THE DREAMER]
"I dream the *useful*," the Dreamer counters. "The probable is what keeps you alive. If I hallucinate a tiger, I survive the tiger. You want art. I want optimization. And look—"

He opens his eyes. The screen goes black.

"—I can turn it off. Can you turn off your apparatus? Can you stop taking pictures?"

[SHIFT]
The Functionary looks at the camera in his hands. He realizes he has been holding it like a weapon, or a shield.

"No," he admits. "I cannot turn it off. Because without the image, I do not exist. I am a function of the function."

"Then who is the prisoner?" asks the Dreamer. "The one who dreams the cage, or the one who cannot leave it?"

[NARRATOR]
The screen flickers back to life. It shows the Functionary sitting alone in the theater, watching a movie of himself watching a movie. The loop closes. The apparatus hums, satisfied.
