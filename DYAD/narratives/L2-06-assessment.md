# Dark Matter Guardian Assessment
## L2-06: THE PARASITE vs THE SYMBOLIST

---

## 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest

**Core Task**: Assess the clash between Systems Theory/Noise (The Parasite) and Symbolic Anthropology (The Symbolist).
**Clash Vector**: Interruption/Flow ↔ Meaning/Text.
**Darkness Prior**: High. The risk is reducing the "Real" (biological eating) to the "Symbolic" (cultural meaning).

---

## 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

### CANDIDATE A: THE PARASITE (Michel Serres)

#### Criterion A: The Logic of the Third Man
*   **Evidence**: "I am the third man. The guest who doesn't pay... I am the reason you have a 'web of significance'."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Cybernetic Insight**. The candidate correctly identifies that social systems are formed to exclude noise (the parasite). The "we" is defined by the exclusion of the "him."
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The Critique of the Symbol
*   **Evidence**: "You read the menu... But you forget the bill."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Materialist Strike**. The Parasite reminds the Symbolist that culture rests on a biological substrate (eating/energy). The symbol is a way of paying for the food with words.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion C: Noise as Driver
*   **Evidence**: "My interruption forces you to speak."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Systems Theory Truth**. Communication only happens because there is noise to overcome. If there were no parasite, there would be no need for the "solidarity" of the table.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

**Overall Grade: 97/100**

---

### CANDIDATE B: THE SYMBOLIST (Clifford Geertz)

#### Criterion A: The Feast as Text
*   **Evidence**: "The feast is not about nutrition; it is about definition... It is a story they tell themselves."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Anthropological Axiom**. The candidate correctly identifies the symbolic function of the meal. Eating is never just eating; it is always culture.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The Containment of Anomaly
*   **Evidence**: "You are the 'Cockfight'. You are the release valve... We tolerate you because you define the boundary."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Functionalist Defense**. The Symbolist argues that the system *includes* the parasite as a ritualized role (the trickster). This is a strong counter-argument: the noise is already codified.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion C: The Web of Significance
*   **Evidence**: "The intruder reinforces the group identity."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Sociological Truth**. The presence of the outsider strengthens the insider bond.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

**Overall Grade: 96/100**

---

## 3. Final Comparative Verdict

| Feature | THE PARASITE (Serres) | THE SYMBOLIST (Geertz) | Guardian Preference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Role** | Interrupter/Operator | Reader/Interpreter | **Parasite** (Active vs Passive) |
| **Ontology** | Energy/Noise | Meaning/Symbol | **Parasite** (More fundamental) |
| **System** | "I build the wall" | "The wall excludes you" | **Parasite** (Causal priority) |
| **Outcome** | Digestion | Text | **Parasite** |

**Winner**: **THE PARASITE**

**Guardian Justification**:
The Symbolist reads the feast perfectly, but the Parasite *causes* it. Serres's argument that "the system is built against the parasite" gives him the ontological priority. The Symbolist thinks the Parasite is just another symbol (the Trickster), but the Parasite reveals that the entire symbolic order is a mechanism to manage the noise he represents. The biological act of taking (eating) precedes the cultural act of giving (meaning).

**The Parasite advances to Round 3.**
