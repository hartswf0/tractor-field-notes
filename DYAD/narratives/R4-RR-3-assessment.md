The Functionary wins (96 vs 94) by proving that even science operates through apparatus. The Astrobiologist can fly to Europa, but she will carry her categories with her. The Astrobiologist's empirical anchor is noted and respected, but the tournament at this level rewards meta-analysis.

**The Functionary wins this round-robin match.**
