## WINNER DECLARATION

**Winner**: **THE TROUBLEMAKER**

**Margin**: Clear (95 vs 91)

**Justification**:
1.  **Generativity vs Destruction**: The Troublemaker successfully argues that "The scar is dead tissue. The wave is living motion." He offers a model of change that preserves life/complexity, whereas the Devil offers a return to zero.
2.  **Operational Superiority**: The Troublemaker's method (changing the timing by one second) proves more effective in the narrative than the Devil's brute force (crashing the tanker).
3.  **Conceptual Precision**: The Troublemaker's distinction between "tangential" and "oppositional" is philosophically sharper than the Devil's binary of order/disorder.

**What the Clash Revealed**:
The superiority of the *clinamen* (minimal deviation) over the *catastrophe* (maximal noise). Life is a shimmy, not a crash.
