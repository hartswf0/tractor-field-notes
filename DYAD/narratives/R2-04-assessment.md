## 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

### CANDIDATE A: THE ASTROBIOLOGIST (Benjamin Bratton)

*   **Evidence**: "The map does not just represent the territory; the map *formats* the territory... We are managing gigatons of flux."

    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The Critique of Localism
*   **Evidence**: "It works for a hut... But we are managing a biosphere... The scale of the problem exceeds the scale of your hand."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Structural Integrity**. The critique of the Foreman's limits is precise. Wittgenstein's "Builders" live in a village. They do not have to manage a biosphere. The Astrobiologist exposes the scaling limit of pragmatism.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion C: The Hyperobject
*   **Evidence**: "You cannot fix the atmosphere with a wrench. You need a lever that moves the *concept*."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Theoretical Innovation**. A strong argument for the performativity of theory. Changing the definition of "Human" or "Climate" *is* a form of terraforming.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

**Overall Grade: 97/100**

### CANDIDATE B: THE FOREMAN (Ludwig Wittgenstein)

#### Criterion A: Meaning as Use
*   **Evidence**: "The meaning of 'Slab' is not in your blueprint... It is in the *bringing*... Don't think, *look*!"
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Philosophical Bedrock**. The Foreman successfully grounds meaning in action. He exposes the Astrobiologist's "Governance" as empty noise unless it connects to a physical operation (turning the valve). The "Don't think, look!" command is the ultimate Wittgensteinian riposte.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: The Critique of Abstraction
*   **Evidence**: "You're looking for the 'essence' of the planet in your data... Your model is just a ghost story."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Anti-Metaphysical Strike**. He diagnoses the Astrobiologist's "Hyperobject" as a grammatical illusion—a "ghost" created by confusing a tool (the concept of climate) with an object.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion C: Operational Reality
*   **Evidence**: "I won't ask for your 'syntax'. I'll just ask for the wrench."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Pragmatic Victory**. In the specific moment of the leak, the Foreman wins. The theory didn't stop the hiss; the wrench did.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

**Overall Grade: 96/100**
