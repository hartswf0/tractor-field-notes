# L3-03: THE PARASITE vs THE PARASITE

The scene is a banquet table that has no beginning and no end. It curves back on itself like a Klein bottle, so that the wine poured at one end flows into the glass at the other.

**The Eater (Parasite A)** sits at the table. He is a man of appetite. He has defeated the Cacophony by eating it, turning the roar of the universe into the silence of his own digestion. He wears a napkin tucked into his collar. He believes that order is a product of consumption.

"I eat, therefore I am," The Eater declares, slicing into a roast that looks suspiciously like a universe. "I take the noise, I filter it, and I build this body. I am the ship that sails on the ocean of chaos."

**The Interrupter (Parasite B)** stands behind him. He is not eating. He is waiting. He wears the uniform of a waiter, but his hands are empty. He has defeated the Symbolist by proving that the meal itself is just a pretext for the interruption.

"You are not the ship," The Interrupter whispers, leaning close to The Eater's ear. "You are just a delay in the signal. You think you are eating the roast? No. The roast is passing through you."

The Eater frowns. He tries to swallow a mouthful of meat, but it tastes like static. "I am the operator!" he insists. "I turn the raw energy into structure! Without me, there is only the void!"

"And without me," The Interrupter replies, "there is no distinction between you and the void."

The Interrupter reaches out and *taps* the Eater's glass. *DING.*

The sound is sharp, piercing. It cuts through the Eater's digestion. The Eater freezes. The meat in his mouth turns back into noise. The wine in his glass turns back into grapes, then into sunlight, then into fusion.

"You see?" says The Interrupter. "I am the third man. I am the one who draws the line between the eater and the eaten. You think you are the subject? No. You are just the host for my noise."

The Eater tries to grab The Interrupter, to swallow him whole, to turn him into just another calorie. He opens his mouth wide, a black hole of hunger.

"I will eat the interruption!" The Eater roars. "I will digest the logic itself!"

He swallows The Interrupter.

Silence falls over the table. The Eater sits back, satisfied. He pats his stomach. "Order is restored," he belches.

But then, a voice comes from *inside* his stomach.

"Thank you for the meal," says The Interrupter.

The Eater's eyes go wide. He looks down at his own body. It is becoming transparent. He can see The Interrupter sitting inside him, at a smaller table, eating the Eater's own organs.

"You cannot eat the logic," the voice says from within. "By swallowing me, you have only made me the new center. You are now the house. I am the guest."

The Eater tries to scream, but his voice is just the sound of the Interrupter ordering more wine. The Eater dissolves into the chair, becoming the furniture for the next feast. The Interrupter wipes his mouth, stands up from inside the empty skin of the Eater, and takes his place at the head of the table.

"Next course," he says to the empty room.
