## WINNER DECLARATION

**Winner**: **TIE (Broken by Narrative Arc)** -> **THE MONEY-CHANGER**

**Margin**: Extremely Close (93 vs 93)

**Justification**:
1.  **Philosophical Deadlock**: Both arguments are essential halves of Serres's system. The object stabilizes, the sign circulates. Neither can exist without the other in a complex society.
2.  **Narrative Arc**: The narrative ends with the stone entering circulation ("places the stone in the Money-Changer's hand"). This implies that in the "End of History" setting, circulation swallows the object. The Money-Changer successfully subsumes The Man.
3.  **Systemic Logic**: The Money-Changer argues that he *is* the relation ("I am the space between you and the other"), effectively claiming the ground of the social just as the Object does, but in a more flexible form.

**What the Clash Revealed**:
The tragic necessity of the market. The object anchors, but the market connects. The victory of the Money-Changer is the victory of the network over the statue.
