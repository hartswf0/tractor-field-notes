# Round 2, Match 5 (L2-05)
## THE PARASITE meets THE CACOPHONY
## The Static and the Storm

**Real Authors**: Michel Serres × Michel Serres

---

[NARRATOR]
The room is filled with static. White noise, pink noise, the roar of a thousand radios tuned between stations.

**THE PARASITE** sits in a chair, eating a piece of cheese. He is the interrupter, the one who breaks the flow to create a new relation. **THE CACOPHONY** is everywhere. It is the background hum of the universe, the sheer multiplicity of things before they are ordered.

[SPEAKER_A: THE PARASITE]
"I am the third man," says the Parasite, chewing. "I interrupt the signal between A and B. By interrupting, I force them to become complex. I am the grit in the oyster that makes the pearl. I am the specific noise that creates the system."

[SPEAKER_B: THE CACOPHONY]
The static swells.

"You are a local event," roars the Cacophony. "You interrupt a conversation. I interrupt *existence*. I am the primal soup from which all systems emerge. You are a operator; I am the background. You steal a meal; I drown the world."

[SPEAKER_A: THE PARASITE]
"But without me," says the Parasite, "you are just noise. You have no meaning. Meaning comes from the *exclusion* of noise. I am the one who draws the line. I say: 'This is the signal, that is the noise.' I am the filter. I turn your chaos into order by eating it."

[SPEAKER_B: THE CACOPHONY]
"You do not eat me," says the Cacophony. "You float in me. You think you are the operator, but you are just a fluctuation in the static. The system tries to exclude me, yes. But I always return. I am the entropy that dissolves your little pearls. You are the arrow of time; I am the heat death."

[SPEAKER_A: THE PARASITE]
"And yet," says the Parasite, pouring himself a glass of wine (stolen). "Here we are, talking. If you were truly the Cacophony, there would be no language. The fact that you are arguing with me proves that I have already won. I have turned your roar into a debate. I have parasitized the void."

[SPEAKER_B: THE CACOPHONY]
The static screams, but it screams in words.

"You trap me in language!" it howls. "You reduce the infinite to a sentence!"

[SPEAKER_A: THE PARASITE]
"That is what life does," says the Parasite. "It steals a little bit of energy from the sun, a little bit of order from the chaos, and it builds a house. I am that house. I am the little pocket of order that survives by eating the disorder. You are the ocean, Cacophony. But I am the ship."

[SHIFT]
The static continues, but it now has a rhythm. It sounds like a heartbeat. Or a digestion.

[NARRATOR]
The Parasite finishes his cheese. The Cacophony continues to roar, but it is now the background music to the Parasite's meal.
