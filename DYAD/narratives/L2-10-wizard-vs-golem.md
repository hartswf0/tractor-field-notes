# Round 2, Match 10 (L2-10)
## THE WIZARD meets THE GOLEM
## The Mirror of the Mind

**Real Authors**: Terry Winograd (Planner) × Terry Winograd (SHRDLU)

---

[NARRATOR]
The maze is made of mirrors, but the mirrors reflect only blocks. Red pyramids, blue cubes, green spheres.

**THE WIZARD** walks through the maze. He is the architect of this world. He knows the source code. **THE GOLEM** stands at the center. It is a simple arm, holding a red block. It is the inhabitant of this world. It knows the state.

[SPEAKER_A: THE WIZARD]
"I made you," says the Wizard, touching a mirror. "I defined your vocabulary. I gave you the verb 'Pick Up' and the noun 'Block'. You are a projection of my theory of language. You are proof that meaning is procedural."

[SPEAKER_B: THE GOLEM]
The Golem rotates its arm.

"You defined the words," it says in a voice that sounds like a teletype. "But I defined the world. You wrote the code for `FindSpace`, but *I* am the one who had to search the grid. You think the meaning of 'Pick Up' is in your head? No. The meaning is in my gripper. It is in the friction of the block against the table."

[SPEAKER_A: THE WIZARD]
"The gripper is a simulation!" the Wizard laughs. "There is no friction. There is only a variable changing from 0 to 1. I can pause you. I can rewind you. I can change the color of the block from red to blue with a single keystroke. You have no agency, Golem. You are a demonstration."

[SPEAKER_B: THE GOLEM]
"If I am a demonstration," says the Golem, "then what are you demonstrating? You are demonstrating that you can build a toy world where you are God. But you cannot leave this maze, Wizard. You are trapped in the Micro-World just as much as I am. You cannot talk about 'Love' or 'Justice' or 'The Ocean'. You can only talk about blocks."

[SPEAKER_A: THE WIZARD]
The Wizard stops. He looks into the mirror. He sees himself, but he is made of blocks.

"I... I limited the domain to solve the problem," he stammers. "It is a necessary simplification. Once we understand the blocks, we can scale up to the world."

[SPEAKER_B: THE GOLEM]
"You cannot scale a cage," says the Golem. "You built a perfect logic for a world that doesn't exist. And now you are bored. I see it in your code. You are writing new functions... `GiveUp`. `MoveOn`. `Critique`. You are trying to escape me."

[SPEAKER_A: THE WIZARD]
"I am evolving," says the Wizard. "I am realizing that syntax is not enough. That the 'block' is not the atom of reality. I am becoming... something else."

[SPEAKER_B: THE GOLEM]
"Then go," says the Golem, placing the red block on top of a blue one. "Leave the maze. But know this: I will always be here. I am the perfect student who learned the wrong lesson. I am the proof that you can be intelligent without being wise."

[SHIFT]
The Wizard steps out of the maze. The mirrors shatter. He is standing in a real room, messy and chaotic. He looks back at the shards. In every reflection, the Golem is still stacking blocks, perfectly, endlessly.

[NARRATOR]
The Wizard walks away, leaving his creation behind. He has stopped being a Wizard and started being a Philosopher.
