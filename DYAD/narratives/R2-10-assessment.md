## 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

### CANDIDATE A: THE HYBRID (Rational Meaning Construction)

#### Criterion A: The Best of Both Worlds
*   **Evidence**: "I let the Large Language Model dream the route, and then I let the Probabilistic Program verify the physics."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Architectural Synthesis**. The candidate correctly articulates the Neurosymbolic promise: LLM for generation (System 1), PPL for verification (System 2). This solves the brittleness of the Navigator and the recklessness of the pure LLM.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: Handling Ambiguity
*   **Evidence**: "The storm is not just a noun. It is a context... It's a metaphor."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Semantic Fluidity**. The Hybrid demonstrates the ability to handle "out of distribution" data (metaphors, unknown tokens) that breaks the formal parser.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion C: The Critique of the Binary
*   **Evidence**: "The universe is not binary. It is probabilistic."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Ontological Accuracy**. The world is messy. A probabilistic approach (sampling) is more robust than a deterministic one (parsing) in a nebula.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

**Overall Grade: 96/100**

### CANDIDATE B: THE NAVIGATOR (Terry Winograd - Programmar)

#### Criterion A: The Logic of Syntax
*   **Evidence**: "My grammar is complete. There is no sentence I cannot diagram."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Formalist Hubris**. The Navigator represents the peak of 1970s AI optimism. He believes that if the syntax is correct, the meaning is captured. History proved him wrong.
    *   *Verdict*: **Shadow**.

#### Criterion B: The Fear of Error
*   **Evidence**: "To be wrong is to die... You rely on the statistical shadow of intelligence."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Valid Critique**. The Navigator points out the real danger of the Hybrid: hallucination ("fly into a star"). Statistical intelligence *is* shadowy. But his alternative (paralysis) is worse.
    *   *Verdict*: **Twilight**.

#### Criterion C: The Crash
*   **Evidence**: "ERROR: UNKNOWN TOKEN... I cannot parse it."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Systemic Failure**. The Navigator fails exactly where GOFAI failed: the edge case, the metaphor, the unknown.
    *   *Verdict*: **Void**.

**Overall Grade: 85/100**
