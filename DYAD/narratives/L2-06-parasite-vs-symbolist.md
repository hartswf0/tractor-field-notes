# Round 2, Match 6 (L2-06)
## THE PARASITE meets THE SYMBOLIST
## The Feast of Signs

**Real Authors**: Michel Serres × Clifford Geertz

---

[NARRATOR]
The banquet hall is loud. It is a wedding feast in a Balinese village, or perhaps a Parisian dinner party. The table is groaning with food—roasted pigs, pyramids of fruit, bottles of wine.

**THE SYMBOLIST** sits at the head of the table. He watches the guests pass the plates. He sees the feast as a text to be read—a complex ritual of hierarchy, solidarity, and meaning. **THE PARASITE** sits under the table, or perhaps at the very edge, uninvited. He is eating a drumstick he stole. He sees the feast as a flow of energy to be intercepted.

[SPEAKER_A: THE SYMBOLIST]
The Symbolist notes the way the wine is poured.

"Observe," he says to the room (though he is really speaking to his notebook). "The distribution of the meat follows the kinship lines. The feast is not about nutrition; it is about definition. By sharing this meal, the village enacts its own social structure. The food is a symbol. The eating is a drama. It is a story they tell themselves about themselves."

[SPEAKER_B: THE PARASITE]
The Parasite burps loudly. The conversation pauses.

"You read the menu," says the Parasite, wiping grease from his chin. "But you forget the bill. The feast is not a story. It is a transfer. The host gives, the guest takes. And I? I take from the guest. I am the noise in your communication. You think this silence is awkward? No. This silence is the *real* event. My interruption forces you to speak."

[SPEAKER_A: THE SYMBOLIST]
"You are an anomaly," the Symbolist says, frowning. "A glitch in the ritual. You have no status here. You are neither kin nor stranger. You are just... taking. You disrupt the web of significance. If I were to analyze you, I would say you represent the 'Anti-Structure'. The chaos outside the village walls."

[SPEAKER_B: THE PARASITE]
"I am the wall!" the Parasite laughs. "There is no system without me. Why do you think they built the wall? To keep me out. Why do they share the food? To keep me quiet. I am the reason you have a 'web of significance' at all. You build your little rituals to exclude the noise, but the noise is what drives you together. I am the third man. The guest who doesn't pay. And because I don't pay, you have to invent 'solidarity' to explain why you haven't kicked me out."

[SPEAKER_A: THE SYMBOLIST]
"We don't kick you out," the Symbolist says, "because the ritual has a place for the trickster. You think you are breaking the system? You are *part* of the system. You are the 'Cockfight'. You are the release valve. Your 'noise' is just another symbol in the lexicon. We tolerate you because you define the boundary of the normal."

[SPEAKER_B: THE PARASITE]
The Parasite steals a bottle of wine.

"Tolerate?" He uncorks it with his teeth. "You feed me. That is the biological reality. You trade your food for my stories. I interrupt, and you listen. I make the system complex. Without me, you are just eating pork in silence. With me, you are having a 'Culture'. I am the operator of the exchange. I eat the food, and I give you the word."

[SHIFT]
The host looks at the Parasite. He looks at the Symbolist. He doesn't know which one is the guest and which one is the pest. He just knows the wine is gone.

"The bill," whispers the Parasite. "Who pays the bill?"

[NARRATOR]
The Symbolist writes: *The intruder reinforces the group identity.*
The Parasite writes nothing. He just digests.
