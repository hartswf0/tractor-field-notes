---

## WINNER DECLARATION

**Winner**: **THE SEMIOLOGIST**

**Margin**: Close (94 vs 92)

**Justification**:
1.  **Framing Dominance**: The Semiologist successfully frames the System-Builder's "nature" as a construction (the glasshouse). The System-Builder admits this ("Perhaps the concept... is a mental isolate"), conceding the semiotic ground.
2.  **The Glasshouse Metaphor**: The setting favors the Semiologist. In a *constructed* environment, the critic of construction has the upper hand over the student of "wild" nature.
3.  **Survival vs Meaning**: The System-Builder argues for survival ("survival imperative"), but the Semiologist counters effectively with "What is the point of survival if we do not know what we signify?"

**What the Clash Revealed**:
The vulnerability of scientific realism in a constructed world. The "ecosystem" is indeed a mental isolate—a useful one, but one that exists within a semiotic frame.
