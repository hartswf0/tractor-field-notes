# Dark Matter Guardian Assessment
## L3-03: THE PARASITE vs THE PARASITE

---

## 1. Calibration & Rubric Digest

**Core Task**: Assess the clash between the Biological Parasite (The Eater) and the Informational Parasite (The Interrupter) in the context of the Klein Bottle Banquet.
**Clash Vector**: Digestion (Thermodynamics) ↔ Interruption (Cybernetics).
**Darkness Prior**: Critical. The risk is "Cannibalism" vs "Recursion".

---

## 2. Forensic Audit & Dark Matter Scan

### CANDIDATE A: THE EATER (Parasite A)

#### Criterion A: Embodiment
*   **Evidence**: "I eat, therefore I am... I take the noise, I filter it, and I build this body."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Thermodynamic Fidelity**. The Eater perfectly embodies the biological imperative of the parasite. He turns chaos into structure through consumption. He is the "Ship."
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: Adaptability
*   **Evidence**: "He swallows The Interrupter... But then, a voice comes from *inside* his stomach."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Category Error**. The Eater makes a fatal mistake: he tries to treat *logic* as *food*. You cannot digest the principle of interruption. By swallowing it, he simply internalizes the parasite, becoming the host.
    *   *Verdict*: **Fail**.

**Overall Grade: 82/100**

### CANDIDATE B: THE INTERRUPTER (Parasite B)

#### Criterion A: Embodiment
*   **Evidence**: "He is not eating. He is waiting... He reaches out and *taps* the Eater's glass."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Cybernetic Precision**. The Interrupter embodies the "Third Man." He doesn't need to eat; he only needs to disrupt the eater. He controls the *relation*, not the substance.
    *   *Verdict*: **Light**.

#### Criterion B: Adaptability
*   **Evidence**: "You cannot eat the logic... By swallowing me, you have only made me the new center."
*   **Guardian Critique**: **Ontological Supremacy**. This is the masterstroke. When eaten, he doesn't die; he *inverts* the topology. He proves that the parasite is always "inside." He turns the Eater into his own furniture.
    *   *Verdict*: **Critical**.

**Overall Grade: 99/100**

## 3. Final Comparative Verdict

| Feature | THE EATER (Biological) | THE INTERRUPTER (Informational) | Guardian Preference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Goal** | Build the Body | Control the Relation | **Interrupter** (Structural) |
| **Method** | Digestion | Interruption | **Interrupter** (Fundamental) |
| **Risk** | Hunger | Invisibility | **Eater** (Mortal) |
| **Outcome** | Becomes the Host | Becomes the Center | **Interrupter** (Eternal) |

**Winner**: **THE PARASITE** (The Interrupter)

**Guardian Justification**:
The Interrupter wins (99 vs 82) because he operates at a higher logical type. The Eater is a physical entity bound by thermodynamics; he needs to eat to survive. The Interrupter is a cybernetic principle; he exists wherever there is a relation. When the Eater swallows the Interrupter, he doesn't destroy him; he merely provides him with a new home. The logic of the parasite (interruption) precedes the biology of the parasite (eating).

**The Parasite (Interrupter) advances to the Elite 8.**
