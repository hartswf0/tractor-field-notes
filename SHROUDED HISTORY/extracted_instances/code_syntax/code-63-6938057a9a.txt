The clean correction in plain words


Sahlins is not “culture matters” in general; he’s making a strong claim that Hawaiian symbolic categories are real causal operators in the historical event. Wikipedia+1

Obeyesekere is not just “Europe projected”; he’s defending native practical reason and attacking the idea that Hawaiians were irrational “believers in gods walking in.” Wikipedia+2ScholarBlogs+2

Geertz, in “Culture War,” reads the whole thing as anthropology arguing about how explanation, evidence, and authority work, not just what happened to Cook. Studocu+1
