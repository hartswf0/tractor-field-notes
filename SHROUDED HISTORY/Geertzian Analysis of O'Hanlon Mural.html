Auditable Record: O'Hanlon Mural

Scope & Limits

Artifact Function Structural analysis of "History of Kentucky" mural via Geertzian (Symbolic) and McLuhanite (Media) frameworks.
Boundaries Limited to the analysis provided in Document d9e2a465. Does not constitute new legal counsel or art historical discovery.
Exclusions Does not adjudicate the moral character of the artist. Does not propose specific remediation designs.

Record

Statement of Fact Source Status
Mural commissioned by PWAP in 1934. 1 Uncontested
Medium is buon fresco (pigment in wet plaster). 2 Uncontested
Depicts enslaved laborers planting tobacco and Indigenous figures with tomahawks. 3 Uncontested
Current legal status involves "Government Speech" doctrine. 4 Contextual
Subject to lawsuit regarding "Public Trust" status (Berry v UK). 5 Judicial Record

Conditions of Production & Use

  • Creator: Ann Rice O'Hanlon (PWAP Artist).
  • Institutional Function (1934): Civic pedagogy; "Naturalization" of state history; production of continuity during Great Depression. 6
  • Constraint: "American Scene" regionalism required depicting labor and local industry. 1
  • Intended Operation: Designed to be walked past; environmental absorption rather than gallery contemplation ("Cool Media"). 7

Visibility & Shrouding

Element Condition
Racial Hierarchy Visible. Enslaved figures positioned as foundational substrate; white figures as surveyors/controllers.
Cultural System (1934) Shrouded. The "webs of significance" that legitimized the imagery as "history" rather than "racism" have collapsed. 8
Physical Removal Obstructed. Fresco medium bonds pigment to structural wall; removal requires destruction. 2
Violence of Slavery Concealed. Omitted in favor of "pastoral industry" mood to maintain civic harmony. 9

Analysis (Bounded)

Issue Governing Standard Application to Record
Mural Meaning Geertz: Art as Cultural System Mural is a "Model Of" (history) and "Model For" (citizenship). Interpretability failed when the 1934 system decoupled from 2024 values. 10
Mural Permanence McLuhan: Bias of Communication Fresco is "Time-Biased" (heavy, permanent). Conflicts with "Space-Biased" (digital, fluid) demands for revision. 11
University Rights Pleasant Grove City v. Summum Mural is "Government Speech." University retains right to control message, but faces physical constraints of the medium. 4

Risks & One-Way Doors

Warning: Irreversibility Detected.

The medium (fresco) dictates that separation from the site is destruction.

Action Nature Reversibility
Removal of Mural Destructive Irreversible (Loss of artifact).
Covering / Boarding Additive / Concealment Reversible (Preserves artifact).
Contextual Install Additive / Dialogue Reversible (e.g., Olivier intervention).

Formal Refusals

  • I refuse to conclude that O'Hanlon's intent was "malicious" or "benevolent." The record supports only that the work functioned to "naturalize" a 1934 hierarchy. 12
  • I refuse to synthesize a "middle ground" between preservation and removal; the medium renders these mutually exclusive physical states.
  • I refuse to interpret the "Warning Label" approach as a cure; Geertzian analysis suggests labels cannot replace a collapsed cultural system.
